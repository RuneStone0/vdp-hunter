Questions

How is the data sourced?

Platform data comes from public program listings and the open bounty-targets-data feed. The self-managed layer comes from our own RFC 9116 crawl, which reads only files organizations publish for exactly this purpose. Sources are attributed on every row.

How fresh is it?

The crawl runs continuously on a 15-minute cycle and upstream platform data refreshes daily. Every API response carries generated_at and the coverage position.

Can I self-host?

Yes. The code is MIT licensed and the repo includes the crawler, the merge pipeline and this API. You supply your own crawl budget.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Cancel any time, and if the dataset is wrong for your use case within the first 14 days, that month is refunded in full.

How do I get a key?

Email us with your use case. Keys are issued the same day during business hours, evaluation keys included.

Is there an SLA?

No separate agreement to sign. The service targets 99.5% monthly uptime for every customer; if it misses, tell us and we make it right.
