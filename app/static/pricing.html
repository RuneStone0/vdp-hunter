Questions

How is the data sourced?

Platform data comes from public program listings and the open bounty-targets-data feed. The self-managed layer comes from our own RFC 9116 crawl, which reads only files organizations publish for exactly this purpose. Sources are attributed on every row.

How fresh is it?

The crawl runs continuously on a 15-minute cycle and upstream platform data refreshes daily. Each API response carries generated_at and the coverage position.

Can I self-host?

Yes. The code is MIT licensed and the repo includes the crawler, the merge pipeline and this API. You supply your own crawl budget.

Do you offer refunds?

Pro is monthly and cancellable any time. If the dataset is wrong for your use case within the first 14 days, we refund the month.

How do I get a key?

Email us with your use case and expected volume. Keys are issued the same day during business hours.

Is there an SLA?

Enterprise only. Pro targets 99.5% monthly uptime with no credit schedule.
